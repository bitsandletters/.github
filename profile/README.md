# Bits&Letters — finally, a website that grows with you

Bits&Letters is a digital agency and design engineering studio. We help growing teams ship ambitious, durable web experiences — untangling the chaos and building systems that just work.

## What we do
- **Build & Scale**: Modern frontend development, headless CMS setups, migrations, and performance-focused foundations that scale with your business.
- **Design Systems**: Accessible, systems-first design; reusable component libraries; rapid prototyping in the browser.
- **Audit & Consult**: Clear answers on performance, architecture, maintainability, and team workflows.

We work with tools and platforms like [Astro](https://astro.build), [Next.js](https://nextjs.org), [Vercel](https://vercel.com), [Sanity](https://sanity.io), [WP Engine](https://wpengine.com), and [Webflow](https://webflow.com).

Along the way, we share as much as we can here as open-source code. Unless otherwise noted, everything in our org is free to use under the MIT license.
